*Settings → Personalisation → Colours → Choose your default app mode → Light*. The app repaints
live. Every palette below was validated arithmetically in both brightnesses and **seen in neither**.

   and never looked at in light), the **float matrix bands**, and the **occupation grid's amber and
   red**.
2. **The readiness strip** — red, under the app bar. `errorContainer` over `onErrorContainer` is the
   pairing most likely to be wrong in a theme nobody has looked at. If nothing is unready, say so

## 2 — Occupation, as it now is

This surface has been rebuilt three times in eight days and the version on screen has been seen
once, briefly. The `Chart | Grid` switch is **gone**: the chart is now the grid's own header through
`PeriodMatrix.banner` — one column width (**72 px**), one frozen gutter, one scrollbar, a bar
directly above its own row of cells.

5. **The chart and the grid are one scroll.** Drag horizontally: a bar must stay over its own
   column, at every unit and every granularity.
6. **Dotted reads as dotted.** The suite asserts where a tick lands, never how a line looks. Are the
   gridlines legible without competing with the **capacity line**, which is the one line this chart
   exists to show bars breaking?
7. **The hours axis is pinned outside the horizontal scroll** — scroll right and confirm it does not
   slide off the left edge. (This was mechanical, not aesthetic: months are 64 px wide.)
8. **Bar tooltips** carry demand, capacity and the process / rework / changeover split. Hover
   several. Recorded against the split and not yet overturned: **rework is 3.4–3.6 % in all fifteen
   months** — a constant wearing a colour.
9. **The row height measures itself** — 60 pt when any row carries a qualifier, 40 pt when none do.
   Switch **Per workcenter** ↔ **Per line** and watch it change. The per-line labels are
   `Fluxo 11B` over `Célula 11`.
10. **All three units.** `%`, **Hours** (demand *stacked over* capacity, not `733/499`), and **Gap**
    (`-234`). Values are centred. Do the numbers stay legible inside a 72 px column at each?
11. **Structural vs order-level — the distinction the whole ticket turns on.** A **structural**
    filter (studies, cells, lines, type, workcenter) moves demand *and* capacity together. An
    **order-level** one (projects, parts, order numbers) leaves capacity fixed, **dims the
    remainder**, and the % label still counts the grey. That is what tells *"CEU27 is 100 % and 80
    of it is yours"* from *"CEU32 is 147 % and none of it is"*. Can you read the difference off a
    cell?
12. **Non-default thresholds.** *Project settings* → the occupation pair beside the float
    thresholds; change **85 / 100** to **70 / 90** (commits on blur). The bands must move. Then try
    a **red below the amber** — it must be *refused*, not stored.
13. **The TOTAL row and column.** TOTAL is along the bottom and always shown; the TOTAL column is
    frozen beside the labels. **Known and unresolved:** under *Per line*, the TOTAL row can equal a
    line's own row character for character, because line rows overlap on shared stations and cannot
    sum. If that reads as a bug rather than as arithmetic, say so — it is fog on the map waiting for
    exactly this.

## 3 — The period slicer and the granularity dropdown

New on 2026-09-07 and **never driven**. The slicer replaced `showDateRangePicker`, which chose
*days* while the button printed only year and month.

14. **The slicer's stops** are the union of capacity months and order months — expect **36** since
    phase 9, up from 15. Drag both ends. The empty range must be **unreachable**.
15. **The dropdown re-columns the matrices** — Year / Semester / Quarter / Month, all four already
    translated. Occupation must fold; **the Delivery Float must not** (its rows are ranks, so
    coarsening would pour 250 orders into one column of 250).
16. **Coarsening hides overload, and that is the measured cost of the control.** The worst station
    reads **148.5 % in a month and 101.8 % in the year**. Month is the default for that reason.
    Switch to Year and back and confirm the peak returns — and say whether the dropdown makes the
    trade visible enough to be safe. **Year is three columns now, not two** (phase 9). Then drag
    the slicer back to the run's own months and confirm the TOTAL column climbs from ≈ 28.5 % to
    ≈ 68.4 %: that is the accepted cost of phase 9 being reversible from the filter bar, and it has
    never been seen.

which is [#10](https://github.com/matheus-sancha/FlowMap/issues/10)'s own named limit. Owed since

**Célula 11C → Demand tab**, 130 orders:

17. **Drag by the row-header number** — the frozen 44 px slot. Nothing inside a cell should be

    **edge auto-scroll** reads at 130 rows — does it run away, stall, or land where you meant?

Then the sort rule — *a surface sorts unless its row order is itself data*:

20. **Sorts, and should:** the study's **Summary** table (arrives alphabetical by station — sort by
    Occupation descending); on **Simulation results → Overview**, the **queue ranking**, the **share
    of flow** and the **parts table**.
21. **The parts table's swatches must not move.** That swatch *is* the Gantt's legend (§8.6). Press
    a heading, confirm **no part changes colour**, then open the **Gantt** and confirm the legend
    still agrees with the bars. This is the trap #10 did not price. *Note while you are there:* 104
    of 146 stored runs exceed eight parts and 76 have 28, so the top eight carry under half a
    28-part Gantt's bars — colour there groups bars rather than naming a part, by decision.

    sequence) and the **Delivery Float** matrix (row *r* means rank *r* in that column). Neither
    heading should respond or show a sort affordance.

## 5 — Navigation, state, and the study side

Sections A and B of the 2026-08-31 sheet, still owed — but two of them are now checks on a **fix**
rather than on an unknown, because [The studies pane reopens on a mode switch](https://github.com/matheus-sancha/FlowMap/issues/18)
swept this neighbourhood and found two defects nobody had reported.

23. **The four study tabs phase 3 never opened.** Stay in **Study** mode and walk **Settings ·
    Capacity · Demand · Summary**. Each must draw something. They are now an `IndexedStack`, so all
    five build on study open — watch for a stall on the first open of a study.
24. **The period control is *hidden*, not greyed**, on Settings, Capacity and Demand, and present on
    **Flow** and **Summary**. That reverses §12.1's old "dimmed rather than hidden" and is
    observable only here. **The strip must not jump** as the control appears and disappears.
25. **State survives a study-tab change** — #18's fix. The **VSM map's zoom and pan** and Demand's
    **Parts | Sequence | MM3** used to reset on every tab change and must not now.
26. **`?study=` across a results-tab change.** Enter the run through a study's own route so the
    location carries `?study=` and the filter arrives pre-set. Change results tab — Overview → Gantt
    → Float — and the filter and the query must both hold. Then **switch to Study mode and back**:
    you must land on the study you left, not on the first one. That second one was a real defect,
    silent, with the right id in the URL throughout.
27. **The studies pane is Study-mode chrome.** It must be **absent in Simulation mode** (its taps
    used to navigate out of it), **present in Project Settings**, and its collapse must survive a
    **restart** — it lives in `window.json` beside `maximized`. Move the window after collapsing it,
    restart, and confirm both stuck.
