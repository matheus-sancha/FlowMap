## Found while driving — the Gantt's row order, `7669856d`

**Reported 2026-08-29 under `0.1.0-2026-08-27a`:** *"in Célula 11D the gantt y axis CEU32 is in
front of CEU30, but this is not the flow order."* It is not, and the cause is **§8.1's phantom
visits** — the defect this drive was scheduled ahead of.

`routingRanks` (`gantt_layout.dart:842`) ranks a station by the earliest **index** it occupies in any
order's step list, after that order's steps are sorted by `queueStart`. A zero-process step occupies
an index while doing nothing, so every station behind it in that routing is pushed one place later.

Replayed against the stored run, counting steps the way the code does:

| | CEU30 | CEU32 | drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| as built | **4** | **4** | tied — the Queue table's busiest-first ranking breaks it, and CEU32 is busier |
| zero-time steps removed | **3** | 4 | **CEU30 first, correctly** |

`7669856d` carries **160 zero-process steps out of 2000**, and **71 of its orders have two steps
sharing one `queueStart`** — a zero-time step ends where it starts, so its successor inherits its
instant exactly. One order, in the run's own figures:

```
CLAD06   proc 94.0 h
CLAD25   proc 78.8 h
TTAT     proc 31.1 h
TCN20    proc  0.0 h   queue 1761776466 -> proc 1761776466 -> end 1761776466
CEU30    proc 94.0 h   queue 1761776466      <- the same instant
CEU32    proc 131.6 h
```

**Two consequences, and both are new.**

1. **§8.1 gains an observable symptom.** It was argued from lane-slot contamination on a capped
   lane, which nothing on screen shows. This is the same bug with a picture: a chart drawing the
   plant's flow in the wrong order. A round justified only by reasoning now has a defect anyone can
   see, which is §2.0's whole argument arriving from the other direction.
2. **A tie in `routingRanks` is broken by a ranking that has nothing to do with the flow.** Even
   after §8.1, two stations genuinely at one position fall to the Queue table's busiest-first order.
   That is right for a pool's like machines and wrong for two stations in sequence. §8.1 removes the
   tie *here*; it does not remove the tie-break. **Recorded, not fixed** — see `TODO.md` §8.1.
