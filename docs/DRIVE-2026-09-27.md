# Windows' text size — does it reach FlowMap, and what breaks under it

The last open ticket on [FlowMap zoom](https://github.com/matheus-sancha/FlowMap/issues/46) (#57).
`AppScale` passes `MediaQuery.textScaler` through untouched, on purpose. The question was whether
Windows' **Settings → Accessibility → Text size** actually reaches that scaler, and if it does, what
it does to screens that #52 had just shown hold at every app scale.

**Nothing was fixed here, deliberately.** The pass-through was an accessibility decision, and what
replaces it is [its own ticket](https://github.com/matheus-sancha/FlowMap/issues/58).

## It reaches the app, and follows it live

**From the engine source** (Flutter 3.44.6, `engine/src/flutter/shell/platform/windows/settings_plugin.cc`):
the embedder reads `HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Accessibility\TextScaleFactor` (a DWORD, percent) and
sends it on the settings channel as `textScaleFactor`. A missing value means 1.0. It also puts a
`RegNotifyChangeKeyValue` watch on the key and sends again on every change.

**On this machine**, with a temporary `debugPrint` inside `AppScale`: with the value at 150,
`MediaQuery.textScalerOf` read `SystemTextScaler (1.5x)`. Setting it to 200 while the app was running
printed `2.0x` with no restart.

So the two scales multiply. 150 % app scale with Text size at 150 % draws text at **225 %**, and
Windows' own slider goes up to 225 % before FlowMap multiplies anything.

## What was driven

Debug build at `e657498`, client area pinned to **1280×720**, Text size at **150 %**, on the live
`VSM 2026 Q1` document with the 17 September run. The app was set to **100 % and 150 %** with its own
control. An 80 % pass happened on the way. Overflow was read from the console, as in #52, through a
temporary `FlutterError.onError` hook that names the source line of *every* overflow. Flutter's own
report names only the first one and summarises the rest as "Another exception".

## Findings

| | screen | 100 % app · 150 % text | 150 % app · 150 % text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | rail, Projects | pass. The rail widens with its labels (158 px) | pass. The rail is 237 px of a 1280 window |
| 2 | **Flow, footer metrics** | **FAIL: every value hidden**, 10 px ×5 | **FAIL**, same |
| 3 | **Flow, canvas step boxes, queues, endpoints** | **FAIL**: 36 px ×7, 14 px ×7, 4 px ×2 | **FAIL**, same |
| 4 | Study Settings, Summary | pass | not re-walked |
| 5 | Capacity | clips silently: `12/31/202`, the `Operators A / B / C` header | pass at the top of the page |
| 6 | Demand | clips silently: part numbers cut inside their cells | pass. 4 rows, 3½ columns, scrolls |
| 7 | results filter bar | pass. Clips, but scrolls, as in #52 | pass, same |
| 8 | Simulation Overview | pass | not re-walked |
| 9 | Production Plan | clips silently: a part number wraps and its second line is cut by the fixed row height | not re-walked |
| 10 | Production Gantt | a group label clipped at the top | not re-walked |
| 11 | Occupation | pass | pass. #54's scroll-as-one takes over, but only ~100 px of pane shows under the run header |
| 12 | **Delivery Float** | **FAIL: every period cell, 2 px ×72** | **FAIL**, same, and the header leaves room for the legend only |

### A — the failures are fixed boxes, and the app scale has nothing to do with them

Every overflow fires with **the same pixel count at 80 %, 100 % and 150 % app scale**:

- `flow_tab.dart:1619` is `_Metric`, in `_FooterMetrics`.
- `:1054` is `_StepBox`.
- `:1292` is `_QueueNode`.
- `:753` is `_Endpoint`.
- `period_matrix.dart:652` is a period cell.

Each one is text in a box whose height was fixed against 1.0× text. The app scale multiplies the box
and the text together, so it cannot cause these failures or cure them. Only the text scale moves one
without the other.

**This is the opposite of #52's result.** The app scale held everywhere because it lies about the
window and leaves every proportion intact. The text scale changes exactly one proportion, and a fixed
box is where that shows.

### B — two kinds of damage, and one of them is silent

The five sites above are **overflows**: striped in debug, and silent clipping in release. The first
two matter most. The footer metrics lose their *values*, the numbers the Flow screen exists to show,
and the canvas boxes lose rows of their data.

Capacity, Demand, the Production Plan and the Gantt **clip without any error**, because their cells
are sized and clipped on purpose. A reader loses characters of a part number or a date and nothing
reports it. No console sweep can find these; only an eye can.

### C — the canvas is a drawing

The VSM canvas already has its own 0.2–3× zoom, and its exports are drawn again by `package:pdf`
without any text scale. A text scale that reaches inside a step box makes the on-screen map disagree
with the map it prints. That matters for the follow-up ticket.

## Not driven

- **Text size above 150 %.** Windows allows up to 225 %. Since every failure is a fixed box, a larger
  scale can only make them worse, never fix them.
- **Compare, the dialogs, light mode, pt/es.** Same gaps as #52.
- **Scrolling inside the 150 % × 150 % results pane**, to confirm the rows are reachable under the
  header.
